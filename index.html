Our Story

A love letter
to the slow ritual.

We started with a stubborn belief — that the simplest things deserve the most care. A croissant should shatter. A latte should hum. A sourdough should taste like the place it was made. Every morning in our Al Rawdah kitchen begins long before the sun rises, with dough rested overnight and beans roasted in small batches just down the road.

Come for the coffee, stay for the kind of stillness Riyadh rarely lets you keep.
